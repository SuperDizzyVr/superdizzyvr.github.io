2. Data we process and why

Meta account information

When the game signs you in with Meta, we receive your app-scoped Meta account ID, Meta username, an app entitlement result and a temporary authentication proof from Meta's platform service. We send the account ID and proof to Meta to verify the sign-in.

We associate the Meta account ID with an internal player ID to restore your game profile and match history across sessions, reinstalls and Quest devices. On the first link, an existing guest profile on that installation can become your Meta-linked profile. If the Meta account already has a profile, we use that profile and do not automatically merge another guest's history.

For a new profile, your Meta username supplies the initial in-game display name. You can edit that name in the game. Existing profiles keep their game name. Your Meta account ID is not displayed to other players.

Guest identity, profile and local settings

Guest play uses a random credential saved on your installation and a separate internal player ID assigned by our service. This is not a hardware serial number or device fingerprint. Our service stores a hash of the credential, your in-game name, profile creation time and last sign-in time. For linked profiles, we also store the Meta account association, session credential hashes and the hashes of retired guest credentials to prevent them from signing in to the linked profile.

The game saves settings, character choices, identity information and pending display-name edits locally so they survive restarts. Settings used to represent your player, such as your chosen character, are also used in multiplayer. Temporary Meta sign-in proofs and active API session credentials are not saved by the game to its local configuration files.

Multiplayer activity and movement

During online play, our game server processes and shares relevant in-game names, character appearance, team membership, positions, head and hand/controller poses, movement inputs, actions and game state with players in the match. This makes avatars, interactions, combat and scoring work consistently. Movement data is used for gameplay, not to identify you through biometric analysis.

We store match records containing player IDs and names, team, kills, deaths, scores, round results and match start/end times to maintain gameplay history. Completed results may also be temporarily queued on the game server until our matchmaking service receives them. The game does not persist a recording of the continuous head and hand movement stream as part of these match records.

Connections, security and support

Our servers process your IP address and connection information to communicate with your device and limit excessive requests. Operational diagnostics include sign-in outcomes, timing and technical errors. If you contact us, we process your email address, message and the information needed to handle your request.

We do not sell player data or use it for advertising. The game does not integrate an advertising or separate analytics service. It does not record microphone audio or camera images, request your contacts or friends list, or use hardware serial numbers to identify players.

3. Legal bases and your choices

Where the GDPR applies, we process data necessary to provide the game and the account and multiplayer features you use under Article 6(1)(b). We process connection security, abuse prevention and necessary troubleshooting data under Article 6(1)(f); our legitimate interests are operating a reliable service and protecting players and accounts. We process data needed to meet legal obligations, including applicable data-rights requests, under Article 6(1)(c).

The website is provided under Article 6(1)(f), based on our interest in making privacy information accessible. Where a processing activity requires your consent, we will request it separately; this policy itself is not a request for consent.

You can use guest play where offered without linking a Meta account to a game profile. Guest play still processes the data needed for online play and does not provide Meta-based profile recovery across devices. Core connection and gameplay data is necessary for online multiplayer. You can use the offline practice area without joining an online match; on Quest, the game attempts Meta sign-in when it starts, so remaining in the practice area alone does not prevent that sign-in request.

4. Who receives data

  • Other players: the name, character, actions and match information needed for multiplayer. We do not include your Meta account ID or authentication credentials in the information displayed to other players.
  • Game hosting: we operate the game and matchmaking services. An external game-hosting provider has not yet been selected. We will update this policy with relevant hosting and transfer information before processing player data with a new provider. The server operator processes the account, connection and gameplay data described above to run the service.
  • Meta: sign-in and entitlement checks use Meta's platform services. Meta processes data about its own accounts, devices and services under its privacy policy. Deleting your game profile does not delete your Meta account.
  • GitHub: this privacy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address and technical request information when you visit. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security, including visits without a GitHub login. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Support and legal recipients: our email service provider processes correspondence needed to handle your requests. We may disclose information to competent authorities where legally required.

This website contains no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded media, external fonts or cookies set by us. Merely visiting it does not send your game profile or Meta account ID to GitHub.

GitHub and Meta may process information outside your country, including in the United States. GitHub describes its transfer safeguards, including standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, in its privacy statement. Meta describes its international transfers in its own policy. For our game hosting and support arrangements: our contact mailbox is provided by Google. Its privacy policy and data transfer information describe its international processing and safeguards. Game-hosting transfer arrangements will be specified before engaging an external provider.

5. How long data is kept

  • Player profiles and match history: retained to maintain your profile and history while needed for that purpose, or until a valid deletion request is fulfilled. There is currently no automatic deletion based on account inactivity.
  • Sign-in data: temporary Meta proofs are processed for verification and are not stored in our account database. API sessions expire after 24 hours; expired session hashes are cleared on subsequent successful Meta sign-ins. Retired guest credential hashes remain with the linked profile until its deletion.
  • Live gameplay: continuous movement and network state is processed in memory for the running game and temporary gameplay buffers. Persisted match summaries are handled as match history.
  • Operational logs: we do not retain server access or diagnostic logs for the game service. Connection information needed for live communication and rate limiting is processed in memory. GitHub's website logging described above is separate.
  • Backups: retained for recovery for a maximum of six months, then deleted or overwritten. A deletion request also applies to personal data in backup copies; the ordinary six-month limit does not postpone fulfilling such a request.
  • Support correspondence: kept while needed to handle your request; after resolution, retained only to the extent necessary for legal obligations or establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.
  • Device settings: remain on your device until you change or clear the app's local data. Clearing local data does not by itself delete server-side records.

Where a legal obligation or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims requires us to retain specific information, we restrict its use to that purpose and retain it only as long as needed. GitHub and Meta set retention for their own services as described in their respective policies.

6. How to request deletion

  1. Email kimkahns@googlemail.com with the subject Delete my Abduction Denied! data.
  2. Give your in-game display name and say whether you used Meta sign-in or guest play. If available, include your app-scoped Meta ID or internal player ID and approximate dates of play to help us locate your records. These IDs are optional for starting a request.
  3. We may ask for limited additional information to verify that the profile belongs to you. Never send your Meta password, authentication proof or session/installation credential. Do not post your request or account details in a public GitHub issue.

After verification, we delete your game profile, Meta association and authentication records and remove or irreversibly anonymise the personal data linked to you in stored match results and pending result uploads, subject to applicable retention exceptions. We also apply the backup handling described above. For a guest profile, lost installation information may limit our ability to identify the correct records; we will explain any such limitation.

We respond without undue delay and normally within one month of receiving your request. If a legally permitted extension is necessary, we inform you within that month and explain why. You do not need a GitHub account to request deletion.

Uninstalling the game does not automatically delete server data. Deleting your game profile does not delete data held independently by Meta or GitHub. Launching the game and signing in or registering as a guest again after deletion can create a new profile.

7. Your rights

If processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Contact us using the details above to exercise your rights. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred. We do not use personal data for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you.

We update this page when our data practices change and revise the date above. Where required, we give additional notice or obtain consent before introducing a new use of your information.
